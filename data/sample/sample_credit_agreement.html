CREDIT AGREEMENT

This Credit Agreement (this "Agreement") is entered into as of January 15, 2024, among Acme Borrower LLC ("Borrower"), the Lenders party hereto, and Example Bank, N.A., as Administrative Agent. This document is entirely synthetic and for testing purposes only.

SECTION 1. DEFINITIONS

"Consolidated EBITDA" means, for any period, Consolidated Net Income for such period plus, without duplication, (a) Consolidated Interest Expense, (b) provision for income taxes, (c) depreciation and amortization expense, (d) non-cash stock-based compensation expense, and (e) extraordinary, non-recurring or unusual losses or charges, in each case to the extent deducted in determining Consolidated Net Income for such period, minus extraordinary, non-recurring or unusual gains.

SECTION 6. FINANCIAL COVENANTS

Section 6.1 Consolidated Leverage Ratio. The Borrower shall not permit the Consolidated Leverage Ratio as of the last day of any fiscal quarter to exceed 3.50:1.00.

Section 6.2 Fixed Charge Coverage Ratio. The Borrower shall not permit the Fixed Charge Coverage Ratio as of the last day of any fiscal quarter to be less than 1.25:1.00.

Section 6.3 Minimum Liquidity. The Borrower shall maintain Consolidated Liquidity of not less than $10,000,000 at all times.

SECTION 7. NEGATIVE COVENANTS

Section 7.1 Indebtedness. The Borrower shall not, and shall not permit any Restricted Subsidiary to, create, incur, assume or suffer to exist any Indebtedness, except (a) the Obligations under this Agreement, (b) Indebtedness existing on the Closing Date and set forth on Schedule 7.1, and (c) purchase money Indebtedness not to exceed $5,000,000 in the aggregate at any time outstanding.

Section 7.2 Liens. The Borrower shall not, and shall not permit any Restricted Subsidiary to, create or suffer to exist any Lien on any property or asset now owned or hereafter acquired, except Permitted Liens.

Section 7.3 Restricted Payments. The Borrower shall not, and shall not permit any Restricted Subsidiary to, declare or make any Restricted Payment, except that the Borrower may make Restricted Payments not to exceed $2,000,000 in the aggregate per fiscal year so long as no Default or Event of Default has occurred and is continuing.
